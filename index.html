The Approach

What Is Visceral Manipulation?

Visceral Manipulation (VM) is a gentle, hands-on therapy developed by French osteopath Jean-Pierre Barral. It works with the body's internal organs — or viscera — and their connective tissue to release restrictions that cause pain, postural problems, and dysfunction throughout the body.

Unlike conventional physical therapy that focuses solely on muscles and joints, VM recognizes that your organs are in constant motion and that restrictions in their movement can create compensatory patterns felt far from the original source.

  • Gentle, non-invasive manual therapy technique
  • Addresses the relationship between organs, fascia, and the musculoskeletal system
  • Treats chronic pain, digestive issues, and post-surgical adhesions
  • Certified through the Barral Institute — the gold standard in VM training
  • Complements traditional physical therapy and other modalities
