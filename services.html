Visceral Manipulation

Signature Service

Visceral Manipulation is a gentle manual therapy that restores organ mobility to relieve chronic pain, post-surgical adhesions, and digestive dysfunction.

  • Chronic back & hip pain
  • Digestive issues
  • Post-surgical adhesions

Visceral Manipulation (VM) is a gentle, hands-on therapy developed by French osteopath and physical therapist Jean-Pierre Barral. The technique works with the body's internal organs — the viscera — and their surrounding connective tissue to evaluate and treat the dynamic relationship between the organs and other structures of the body.

Organs are not static. They move with every breath, heartbeat, and postural change. When an organ loses its normal mobility due to illness, injury, surgery, or emotional stress, it creates tension patterns that radiate through the body — often manifesting as pain far from the original restriction.

Dr. Friedman uses exquisitely sensitive palpation to detect these restrictions and applies precise, gentle pressure to release them — restoring normal organ mobility and resolving the downstream pain patterns they create.

Conditions Addressed

  • Chronic lower back & hip pain
  • Digestive disorders (IBS, GERD, constipation)
  • Post-surgical adhesions & scar tissue
  • Pelvic pain & dysfunction
  • Shoulder & neck pain
  • Urinary incontinence & bladder issues
  • Acid reflux & hiatal hernia
  • Endometriosis-related pain
  • Liver & gallbladder dysfunction
  • Thoracic outlet syndrome
  • Unexplained chronic pain
  • Breathing restrictions

Neural Manipulation

Advanced Technique

Neural Manipulation helps restore nerve mobility to reduce pain, numbness, and improve function.

Neural Manipulation (NM) is a complementary technique, also developed by Jean-Pierre Barral in collaboration with Alain Croibier, that assesses and treats the mechanical and neurological dysfunction of the nervous system. Nerves need to move freely within their surrounding tissue — when they don't, the result can be pain, numbness, weakness, and a wide range of neurological symptoms.

Dr. Friedman applies gentle, specific pressure along nerve pathways to release fixations and restore normal neural mobility. The effects are often immediate and profound — patients frequently report relief from symptoms they have carried for years.

Neural Manipulation is particularly effective for conditions where conventional treatments have failed to provide lasting relief, as it addresses the nervous system component that is often overlooked.

Conditions Addressed

  • Headaches & migraines
  • Carpal tunnel syndrome
  • Sciatica & radiculopathy
  • Trigeminal neuralgia
  • Thoracic outlet syndrome
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • TMJ dysfunction
  • Post-concussion syndrome
  • Peripheral neuropathy
  • Tinnitus & vertigo
  • Chronic nerve pain
  • Whiplash-associated disorders
