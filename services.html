Visceral Manipulation

Visceral Manipulation (VM) is a gentle, hands-on therapy developed by French osteopath Jean-Pierre Barral. It works with the body's internal organs — the viscera — and their surrounding connective tissue to release restrictions that radiate pain far from their origin.

Unlike conventional physical therapy that focuses solely on muscles and joints, VM recognizes that your organs are in constant motion. When an organ loses mobility through illness, injury, or stress, the compensatory tension it creates can show up as hip pain, back pain, digestive dysfunction, or headaches — seemingly unrelated symptoms with a single mechanical cause.

Dr. Friedman holds certifications VM1 through VM5 from the Barral Institute — the most rigorous VM training pathway in the world.

Neural Manipulation

Neural Manipulation (NM) assesses and treats the mechanical and neurological dysfunction of the nervous system. Developed by Jean-Pierre Barral and Alain Croibier, it rests on a simple principle: nerves must move freely within their surrounding tissue to function properly.

When a nerve is fixated — tethered by scar tissue, compressed by a restricted organ, or caught in fascial adhesion — the results range from localized pain and numbness to systemic symptoms that have baffled every specialist. Dr. Friedman applies gentle, precise pressure along nerve pathways to release these fixations and restore normal neural mobility.

The effects are often immediate. Patients frequently report relief from symptoms carried for years, resolved in a single session.

Is This Right for You?

Who Can Benefit

Our approach is especially powerful for patients who have been everywhere and found nothing that lasts.

01

Unresolved Chronic Pain

You've tried chiropractic, massage, injections, cortisone — nothing lasts. Visceral and neural manipulation reaches the structural origin that all other approaches bypass entirely. Many patients experience more change in two sessions than in years of prior treatment.

02

Post-Surgery Recovery

Surgical adhesions and scar tissue are invisible on imaging but profoundly disruptive to organ mobility and fascial continuity. VM is highly effective at releasing these adhesions and resolving the persistent pain that follows procedures months or years after they heal on the surface.

03

Digestive Dysfunction

IBS, GERD, chronic constipation, and bloating often have a mechanical component that gastroenterology never addresses. When an organ's motility is restored, the functional symptoms it was creating frequently resolve without medication changes or dietary restriction.

04

Neurological Symptoms

Numbness, tingling, nerve pain, tinnitus, and persistent headaches that don't respond to standard care almost always have a neural restriction component. NM releases the specific fixation point — often surprisingly distant from the symptom site.

Understanding the Difference

Visceral vs Neural Manipulation

Both techniques share the same philosophy — restore mobility, resolve pain — but target different anatomical systems. Most patients benefit from both.

Visceral Manipulation Neural Manipulation
Primary Target Internal organs & connective tissue Peripheral nerves & cranial structures
Developed By Jean-Pierre Barral Barral & Alain Croibier
Best For Organ-referred pain, digestive dysfunction, post-surgical adhesions Nerve pain, headaches, numbness, neurological symptoms
Pressure Used Extremely gentle — grams of force Light & precise — no manipulation
Typical Sessions 4–8 sessions for most conditions 3–6 sessions; often faster response
